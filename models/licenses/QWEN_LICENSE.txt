ARCHIVED LICENSE COPY — Qwen models
===================================

This file is the archived license reference for Qwen3 models admitted to the
JOL registry (Qwen3-32B, Qwen3-235B-A22B). Per
docs/05-models/license-compliance.md, the authoritative text is captured
verbatim at model admission time; this placeholder is replaced with the
verbatim upstream text during the first kiosk import of each model.

Canonical source at admission:
  https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-32B/blob/main/LICENSE

Summary recorded by the license audit (NOT a substitute for the full text):

- Grant: free commercial and non-commercial use, subject to conditions.
- Attribution: copyright notice and license must accompany redistributions.
- Restriction: the models and derivatives may not be used to train
  competing large language foundation models.
- Acceptable-use clause applies (no unlawful or harmful use).
- MAU threshold clause: not applicable at JOL's internal scale; re-verify
  if the platform is ever exposed beyond internal users.

Audit: 2026-07-20, security. See docs/05-models/license-compliance.md.
